## Three optimization windows and invariants

1. **CombatEffects.Update / EffectBatch submission:** largest stable presentation window. Inspect repeated native object creation and geometry submission/batching. Preserve vertices, ordering, UVs, HDR colors, alpha, timing, randomness and visibility. Reducing particles, effects or shader workload is not an equivalent CPU optimization.
2. **Vehicle.Step and its suspension/leg/ray math:** largest stable simulation window and dominant measured Lua allocation window. Inspect repeated query/matrix/table/vector preparation. Preserve ray query order/masks, contacts/normals, 60 Hz forces/torques, controls, damage and motion. Do not reduce physics or AI update frequency.
3. **CombatView/DriveView/WeaponView/idle property submissions:** avoid only proven unchanged native properties or repeated derived preparation. Preserve interpolation, wheels/legs/rotors/turrets, debris, hit flash, restored/damaged parts, and rebinding to a new vehicle after repair/rebuild/respawn.

No optimization has been implemented or visually accepted by this diagnostic. Fine-grained timing around every ray/vertex heavily perturbs work and is excluded from final hotspot claims.
