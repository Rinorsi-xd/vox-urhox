Production invalidation audit:

- `Vehicle:ApplyRotors` is the identified original in-place geometry writer,
  applying `RotorMotion.Follow` to centers and quaternions. Nonempty or absent
  `vehicle.rotors` always uses `OriginalBounds` and clears the private cache.
- `CarUnit:Flush` after damage or structure repair builds a replacement spec,
  disposes the previous Vehicle and constructs a new Vehicle. Cache state belongs
  to that Vehicle and cannot cross the rebuild. `Spawn`, respawn and refit use
  the same disposal/new-instance route.
- Legs and mech hitboxes still invalidate `rayBounds` after each hitbox update;
  the candidate re-unions all current dynamic segments and wheel/drop bounds.
  Those moving hitboxes are never cached as static geometry.
- Spec/list identity and list-count changes clear static extrema at the next
  Bounds reconstruction. An arbitrary external in-place no-rotor shape mutation
  retaining all identities/counts remains outside the cache contract. No such
  production writer was found in the reviewed source searches.
- Existing full `rayBounds` validity rules are retained. Changing a shape list
  without invalidating an already-present full `rayBounds` was already unsupported
  by the original function; the new private cache does not add that invalidation.

### Usable allocation attribution within the detailed arm

The paired rejection does not invalidate the detailed arm's internally conserved
allocation accounting. `analyze-gc-ray-allocation-detail.cjs` separately verifies
that one arm, without subtracting its unmatched baseline. The derived artifact is
`evidence/gc-ray-allocation-next-native-1017-a-detail.json`.

Across its eight actual samples, VehicleRay.Cast is called 308 times and allocates
106.391 KiB/step inclusive. Sixteen calls enter without cached bounds and allocate
50.609 KiB/step; 292 calls enter with cached bounds and allocate 55.766 KiB/step.
Cold calls therefore account for about 47.57% of measured ray allocation, not a
majority, while occurring far less frequently. The cold rows still include full
intersection work, so they do not measure Bounds exclusively.

With that missing child boundary added, TrackWorld.CastRay's self allocation in
this arm is only 0.267 KiB/step. This supports prioritizing the analytic vehicle
ray path over TrackWorld's loop itself. Math3 Rotate self is 26.343 KiB/step,
Unrotate self 14.047, Sub 6.602, Scale 4.240 and Add 2.349, all limited to the
dynamic VehicleRay call stack; these are allocation bytes, not recoverable frame
time. Unrotate inclusive overlaps Rotate and must not be added to it.

There is a concrete cache invalidation constraint: Vehicle.ApplyRotors calls
RotorMotion.Follow on spec.boxes/hulls/sensors, updating their center/quaternion
in place, and clears rayBounds. Therefore those three spec lists are **not**
universally immutable. Any static-spec extrema candidate must retain the original
path for vehicles with turntables/rotors. CarUnit.Build creates a new Vehicle for
a new spec, and damage Flush disposes the old Vehicle before rebuilding; those
paths must be covered by a candidate test before adoption. A narrow no-rotor
static-spec extrema experiment is being prepared separately, with no production
change or claimed performance benefit.
